# Privacy Policy - Anime Legends: Eternal Awakening
Developer: Poyraz Yazılım - omerr.poyrazz@gmail.com - Last updated: 6 November 2026

**English.** The game is single-player and works offline. Your progress, settings and purchases state are stored only on your device. We do not run servers, accounts or analytics and we do not collect, sell or share personal data. If advertising is enabled in your version, ads are provided by Google AdMob which may process your advertising ID, IP address and device information under Google's privacy policy; you can reset or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android/iOS settings. Purchases are processed by Google Play / Apple; we never see payment details. The game is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. To delete your data, uninstall the game or use Settings > Reset. Contact: omerr.poyrazz@gmail.com.
